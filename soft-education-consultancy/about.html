Who We Are

Helping You Make Informed, Confident Academic Decisions

SOFT Education Consultancy was founded to bridge the gap between aspirational students and genuine higher education institutions. We understand that choosing a course or career path is among the most pivotal decisions of a person's life.

Every student has unique interests, qualifications, and financial considerations. Our method starts with active listening, identifying your real requirements, and mapping out practical, transparent next steps without any pressure or false claims.
