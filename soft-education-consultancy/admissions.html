📚 Library & Information Science Programs

UGC Approved Degrees
Bachelor • 1 Year

BLIS

Bachelor of Library and Information Science
Duration 1 Year (2 Semesters)
Eligibility Graduation in any stream (B.A, B.Sc, B.Com, BCA, etc.)
  • Qualifies for Government School/College Librarian vacancies (KVS, NVS, DSSSB)
  • Training in Digital Libraries, Koha software, Cataloguing & Classification
  • Available in Regular & Approved Distance/Open University modes
  • High placement rate with low competition compared to traditional degrees

Master • 1 Year

MLIS

Master of Library and Information Science
Duration 1 Year
Eligibility BLIS / B.Lib.I.Sc from recognized university
  • Essential qualification for University Assistant Librarian & Chief Librarian posts
  • Eligible for UGC-NET (Library & Info Science) and Assistant Professor roles
  • Advanced digital resource curation, metadata architecture & research management
  • Lucrative careers in national archives, research institutions & corporate knowledge centers
